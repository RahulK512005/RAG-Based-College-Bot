# Global Institute of Technology & Management - Academic Fee Structure (Academic Year 2026-2027)

## 1. Annual Tuition Fees by Program
Tuition fees are payable per academic year in two equal semester installments.

- B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):
  - Annual Tuition Fee: INR 1,85,000 per year
  - Semester Installment: INR 92,500
- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS):
  - Annual Tuition Fee: INR 1,75,000 per year
  - Semester Installment: INR 87,500
- B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):
  - Annual Tuition Fee: INR 1,50,000 per year
  - Semester Installment: INR 75,000
- B.Tech in Mechanical / Civil Engineering:
  - Annual Tuition Fee: INR 1,25,000 per year
  - Semester Installment: INR 62,500
- Master of Business Administration (MBA):
  - Annual Tuition Fee: INR 2,20,000 per year

## 2. One-Time Charges (Payable at Admission)
- Admission Registration Fee: INR 5,000 (Non-refundable)
- Institutional Caution Deposit: INR 10,000 (Refundable upon course completion)
- Alumni Association Lifetime Membership: INR 3,000
- Library & Digital Resources Fee: INR 7,000 (One-time)

## 3. Examination and Laboratory Fees
- Semester Examination Fee: INR 2,500 per semester
- Advanced Computing / Innovation Lab Fee: INR 4,000 per semester

## 4. Payment Modes & Late Fine Policy
- Accepted payment methods: Net Banking, NEFT/RTGS, UPI, and Demand Draft favoring "GITM Academic Fund".
- Due date for Odd Semester fee: August 10, 2026.
- Due date for Even Semester fee: January 15, 2027.
- Late Fee: INR 100 per day after the due date up to 15 days, after which student registration will be temporarily suspended.

## 5. Fee Refund Policy
- 100% refund (less INR 1,000 processing fee) if withdrawal notice received 15 days prior to orientation.
- 80% refund if withdrawal within 15 days after orientation.
- No refund of tuition fee after 30 days from the start of academic classes.
