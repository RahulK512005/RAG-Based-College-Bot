# Global Institute of Technology & Management - Hostel Residence Manual & Code of Conduct

## 1. Accommodation Options & Fee Structure
Hostel accommodation is available on campus for boys and girls in separate residential blocks.
- Non-AC 3-Sharing Room (Attached Bathroom): INR 75,000 per academic year
- Non-AC 2-Sharing Room (Attached Bathroom): INR 90,000 per academic year
- AC 2-Sharing Deluxe Room: INR 1,20,000 per academic year
- AC Single Occupancy Room (Senior/PG Students): INR 1,50,000 per academic year
- Mess Food Fee (4 meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, High Tea, Dinner): INR 45,000 per year (Mandatory for all hostel residents).
- Refundable Hostel Caution Deposit: INR 5,000.

## 2. Hostel Timings and Gate Curfew
- Main Hostel Gate Curfew Time: 9:00 PM on weekdays (Monday through Friday), 10:00 PM on weekends.
- Night Roll Call Attendance: Conducted daily at 9:30 PM by the resident hostel warden.
- Late Entry Permission: Prior approval from the Chief Warden via digital gate pass is mandatory for late returns due to academic projects or emergency reasons.
- Night Out / Leave Policy: Written parent consent via registered email/SMS must be submitted 24 hours prior to departure.

## 3. Amenities and Campus Facilities
- 24/7 High-Speed Wi-Fi (1 Gbps fiber backbone).
- Modern Gymnasium with certified fitness instructors.
- Indoor Sports Complex (Badminton courts, Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom).
- Automated Laundry Services (3 loads per week included in hostel fee).
- On-Campus Health Clinic with full-time doctor and 24-hour emergency ambulance.

## 4. Disciplinary Regulations
- Strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging, harassment, alcohol, smoking, or unauthorized electrical appliances (heaters/induction stoves).
- Violation results in immediate hostel suspension and academic disciplinary committee review.
