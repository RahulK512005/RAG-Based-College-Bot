# Global Institute of Technology & Management - Scholarship & Financial Aid Schemes

## 1. Merit-Based Entrance Scholarships
- Category 1 (JEE Main Rank 1 to 5,000 or SEEE Rank 1 to 100):
  - 100% Tuition Fee Waiver for all 4 years.
- Category 2 (JEE Main Rank 5,001 to 15,000 or SEEE Rank 101 to 500):
  - 75% Tuition Fee Waiver for the first year.
- Category 3 (Class 12th Board PCM >= 95% aggregate):
  - 50% Tuition Fee Waiver for the first year.

## 2. Need-Based & Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Financial Aid
- Family annual income below INR 3,00,000: Up to 50% tuition fee concession upon submission of government income certificate and asset verification.
- Single Parent / Orphan Student Grant: 50% tuition fee waiver and subsidized hostel accommodation.

## 3. Women in Technology & Diversity Scholarship
- "Pragati Tech Fellowship": INR 50,000 annual scholarship awarded to top 30 female engineering students entering CSE, AI/DS, and ECE programs.

## 4. Sports & Extra-Curricular Excellence Scholarship
- National-level medalists in recognized sports: 100% sports scholarship + free hostel.
- State-level medalists: 50% sports scholarship.

## 5. Renewal Criteria for Continuous Scholarships
- Students must maintain a minimum SGPA / CGPA of 8.5 at the end of each academic year.
- No history of disciplinary misconduct or attendance shortage below 75%.
