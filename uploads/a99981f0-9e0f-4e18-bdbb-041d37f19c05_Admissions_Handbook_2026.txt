# Global Institute of Technology & Management - Official Admissions Handbook 2026

## 1. Undergraduate Engineering Programs (B.Tech)
Global Institute of Technology offers 4-year full-time B.Tech programs in the following disciplines:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 180 seats
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS): 120 seats
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): 120 seats
- Mechanical Engineering (ME): 60 seats
- Civil Engineering (CE): 60 seats

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for B.Tech Admissions 2026
Candidates seeking admission to the First Year B.Tech degree program must satisfy the following:
1. Passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as compulsory subjects.
2. Secured an aggregate minimum of 60% marks in PCM (55% for SC/ST/OBC category candidates).
3. Qualified in JEE Main 2026 or State Engineering Entrance Examination (SEEE-2026).
4. Age Requirement: Candidate must have attained 17 years of age as of December 31, 2026.

## 3. Application Process and Key Deadlines
- Online Application Portal Opens: March 15, 2026
- Last Date for Submission of Online Application: June 10, 2026
- Counseling Round 1 (Merit List Announcement): June 20, 2026
- Document Verification & Seat Confirmation: June 25 to June 30, 2026
- Commencement of Orientation Program: August 1, 2026

## 4. Required Verification Documents
At the time of admission counseling, candidates must produce original copies of:
- Class 10th and 12th Marksheets and Passing Certificates.
- JEE Main / SEEE Score Card 2026.
- Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate from the previous institution.
- Category / Caste Certificate (if claiming quota reservation).
- 6 recent passport-size color photographs.
- Government-issued Identity Card (Aadhaar Card / Passport).
