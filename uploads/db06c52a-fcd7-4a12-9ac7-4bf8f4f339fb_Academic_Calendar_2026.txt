# Global Institute of Technology & Management - Official Academic Calendar (2026-2027)

## 1. Odd Semester (Fall 2026) Schedule
- Student Registration & Fee Payment: July 25 – July 31, 2026
- Orientation & First Year Induction: August 1 – August 5, 2026
- Commencement of Regular Classes: August 6, 2026
- First Mid-Semester Examination (Mid-Term 1): September 21 – September 26, 2026
- Annual Technical Fest "TECH-INNOVA 2026": October 15 – October 17, 2026
- Second Mid-Semester Examination (Mid-Term 2): November 9 – November 14, 2026
- Last Instructional Day of Classes: December 4, 2026
- Practical & Laboratory Examinations: December 7 – December 12, 2026
- End-Semester Final Theory Examinations: December 14 – December 30, 2026
- Winter Vacation: January 1 – January 14, 2027

## 2. Even Semester (Spring 2027) Schedule
- Semester Reopening & Course Registration: January 15, 2027
- Commencement of Classes: January 18, 2027
- Cultural Fest "RHYTHMS 2027": February 26 – February 28, 2027
- Mid-Semester Examinations: March 15 – March 20, 2027
- Last Day of Classes: April 30, 2027
- End-Semester Examinations: May 10 – May 28, 2027
- Summer Internships / Break: June 1 – July 20, 2027

## 3. Attendance Policy
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory and laboratory classes is strictly required to be eligible for End-Semester Examinations.
- Medical leave concession up to 10% is permissible upon submitting a valid hospital medical certificate endorsed by the Chief Medical Officer within 3 days of resuming classes.
